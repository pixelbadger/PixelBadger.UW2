Ultima Underworld II

Labyrinth of Worlds, running on a web engine

Choose your Ultima Underworld I & II disc image (.iso), or the files from its UW2/DATA folder. The game data is read here in your browser and kept on this device for next time; nothing is uploaded.

You can also drop the file anywhere on this page. Walk every level with its original textures, lighting, doors, stairs and ladders. Creatures are animated and wander about, and books, letters and signs can be read. You can pick things up and carry them, and talk with the people you meet. You can trade with them too. Combat and spells are not built yet.
